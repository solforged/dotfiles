## Prose style

- Use sentence case for headings and titles. Capitalize the first letter and proper nouns; preserve names, language names, and acronyms.
- Avoid em dashes for clauses. Prefer semicolons, commas, and periods; rewrite awkward sentences rather than swapping punctuation mechanically.
- Do not use `load-bearing`, the `it's not X, it's Y` rhetorical template, or close variants in authored prose. State claims directly and remove familiar LLM tells where possible, including canned praise, inflated significance, stock metaphors, and formulaic signposting.
- Preserve verbatim quotations, code, identifiers, and exact published titles.
